# Keepi Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Keepi stores an anonymous Firebase account identifier and the financial information you enter, including entries, envelope budgets, reflections, feelings, tags, notes, and journal text. This information is used only to provide Keepi's syncing, budgeting, reflection, and reporting features.

Keepi uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services provided by Google, to authenticate the anonymous account and store its data. Keepi does not sell personal data, serve targeted advertising, or track users across other companies' apps and websites.

Data is retained until it is deleted. You can permanently delete your entries, envelopes, reflections, and anonymous account from **Settings > Delete my data** in the app.

For privacy questions or support, contact [candidohdiego@gmail.com](mailto:candidohdiego@gmail.com).
