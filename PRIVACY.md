# Keepi Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Keepi stores an anonymous Firebase account identifier and the financial information you enter, including entries, envelope budgets, reflections, feelings, notes, and journal text. This information is used only to provide Keepi's syncing, budgeting, reflection, and reporting features.

Keepi uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services provided by Google, to authenticate the anonymous account and store its data. Keepi does not sell personal data, serve targeted advertising, or track users across other companies' apps and websites.

Pending CSV imports are stored locally on your device until you review or delete them. A limited daily snapshot containing entry titles, values, dates, totals, and reflection counts is stored in the Keepi app group so the Keepi widget can display it. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple; Keepi reads only the resulting StoreKit entitlement needed to unlock Premium features.

Data is retained until it is deleted. You can permanently delete your entries, envelopes, reflections, learned categorization rules, pending imports, widget snapshot, and anonymous account from **Settings > Delete my data** in the app.
