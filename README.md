# Keepi - Your Spending Journal

Keepi is a new kind of financial app. It’s not just about tracking numbers, budgets, or investments; it’s about understanding your relationship with your money. Keepi is a **Spending Journal** designed to help you understand your spending habits, discover what drives your purchases, and identify meaningful patterns in your financial life.

*Understand your spending, not just track it.*

---

## 🌟 Full Feature Breakdown

### 📖 The Spending Journal (Core)
The transaction is basically your journal entry. Keepi replaces traditional expense tracking with mindful journaling. Every time you make a purchase, you're encouraged to reflect on it.
- **Journal Entries**: Add text explanations to your spending to remember the context and motivation behind it.
- **Entry Types**: Categorize as Expense, Income, or Transfer.

### 💖 Emotional Reflection
Every transaction asks you a series of mindful questions:
- *How did this purchase feel?* (Using distinct feeling indicators)
- *Was it planned?*
By answering these questions, you gain deep insights into what truly makes you happy and what spending causes stress or regret.

### 🧠 Meaningful Patterns
Keepi starts finding patterns in your spending behavior so you don't have to. You'll get insights like:
- "You spend the most when you feel sad."
- "70% of your unplanned purchases were regretted."
- "Purchases made while stressed are often regretted."
This helps you identify triggers and make proactive changes to your financial life.

### ✉️ Envelope Organization
Keep your primary finances organized without the overwhelming complexity of traditional budgeting apps.
- **Visual Envelopes**: Categorize your entries into different Envelopes (e.g., Groceries, Fun, Bills).
- **Budget Tracking**: See how much you have left in each envelope at a glance.

### 📊 Reflection & Reports
- **Weekly & Monthly Summaries**: View your spending trends over time.
- **Feeling Distribution**: See a breakdown of how your spending made you feel.
- **Planned vs. Unplanned**: Track your impulsivity.

### 🔒 Secure & Private
Keepi stores an anonymous account identifier, your entries, envelope budgets, reflections, feelings, and journal texts securely in Firebase. Your data is synced to the cloud so you never lose your journal, while respecting your privacy.

## Getting Started

**This repository contains the source code for the iOS app. The app is available on the App Store.**

### Installation

1. Clone the repository:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/DHenrickCandido/keepi.git
```
2. Open the project in Xcode (`Keepi.xcodeproj`).
3. Ensure you have the necessary dependencies installed (e.g., Firebase).
